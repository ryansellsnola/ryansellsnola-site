About This Home

Available now in the heart of vibrant Mid-City, just one block from the Canal Street streetcar line. This 2-bedroom, 1-bath upper unit sits in a historic 1938 building that has been updated where it matters most, with major mechanicals already replaced.

Inside you will find warm wood floors in the living room and kitchen, granite countertops, and stainless steel appliances including a dishwasher and microwave. A full-size washer and dryer are in the unit and yours alone. This is a classic New Orleans shotgun layout, so the rooms connect directly to one another and you pass through the first bedroom to reach the back of the unit.

Out back there is a shared courtyard and covered porch. You are just blocks from Bayou St. John, with City Park and Mid-City's best restaurants and coffee shops close by, and the streetcar right there for an easy ride downtown. The property sits near the Endymion parade route.

Pets are welcome. Schedule your showing today and make this one yours.
