About This Home

Welcome to 733 Guardian, a three bedroom, two full bath brick ranch on a large corner lot where the square footage on paper does not tell the whole story. Past the 1725 feet of main living space, a 40 foot sunroom adds another 666 square feet of year round room under a wood plank ceiling and exposed beam, the kind of space that turns into a second den, a playroom, or wherever everyone ends up on a Sunday afternoon.

An indoor garden runs the length of the windows in the sunroom, adding a lush green space you would not expect to find inside. There is a built in bar just off the kitchen and living area, brand new refrigerator and dishwasher, and a backyard with a hot tub that stays and a 220 outlet already wired for a camper or RV.

Two car attached garage, mature trees, Flood Zone X. Real space in a highly desirable Terrytown neighborhood. Call to schedule your showing.
